YOUR WEBSITE STARTS HERE
=======================

1. Extract this template ZIP. Keep the files together.
2. Open editor/index.html in your browser.
   On Windows, you can double-click OPEN EDITOR.cmd instead.
3. Replace the example profile, projects, writing and notes.
4. Choose a theme and check which entries to include.
5. Click Save backup. Keep this editable JSON copy safe and private.
6. Click Download website.
7. Upload that website ZIP to a static hosting provider.

No Python, GitHub, Netlify or OAuth setup is needed for the editor.
You need a hosting account and internet access to put the site online.

For updates: reopen the editor, open your backup, edit, save a new
backup, download a new website ZIP and replace the online deployment.

Read docs/BEGINNER_SETUP.md for help.
