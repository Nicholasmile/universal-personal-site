How do I publish?
  1. Save a backup. It keeps your editable work, including drafts.
  2. Download your website ZIP. It contains only published content in visible sections.
  3. Upload the ZIP to a static host, such as Cloudflare Pages Direct Upload, or upload its extracted files to your hosting provider.
  4. For updates, reopen this editor, open your backup, edit, and upload a new website ZIP.

Your website ZIP does not include this editor or your backup. “Published” includes an entry in the download; it does not put it online automatically.
